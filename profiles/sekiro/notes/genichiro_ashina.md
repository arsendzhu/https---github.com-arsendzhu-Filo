# Genichiro Ashina
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Genichiro_Ashina,_Second_Encounter
tags: boss, ashina castle, lightning, way of tomoe, deflect

The first Genichiro encounter, right at the start, is a fight you are meant to lose: winning it only slightly changes the cutscene. Fight near the bridge on the dirt road so the tall grass does not hide him, stay close so he does not use his bow, and jump onto his Perilous Sweep for big posture damage. His Perilous Grab is heavily telegraphed and leaves him open afterwards.

The real fight is at the Castle Tower Lookout on top of Ashina Castle, in three phases. Phases 1 and 2 are pure swordsmanship and archery: deflect his combos (Floating Passage is a seven-hit chain ending in a guard-breaking strike), Mikiri Counter his Perilous Thrusts, jump his Perilous Sweeps, and expect a guard-breaking arrow whenever you heal. He is often open after his jumping overhead: crouching means a thrust is coming, a clockwise turn means a sweep.

Phase 3 is the Way of Tomoe: he opens with a long lunging thrust and adds lightning arrows and lightning sword strikes that inflict Shock. The counter is Lightning Reversal: jump as the lightning attack comes, take the lightning while airborne, and attack before landing to throw it back for heavy damage. Ako's Sugar, Ungo's Sugar and Gokan's Sugar all help a lot here, and the Loaded Axe deals big damage.
