# Chained Ogre
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Ogre
tags: mini-boss, ashina outskirts, fire, grabs, stealth

The Chained Ogre is a mini-boss shackled at the top of the stairs past the Outskirts Wall - Stairway idol (another appears later in Ashina Castle). Its red eyes mean it is terrified of fire: the Flame Vent from the Hirata Estate scares it for an opening, and a second blast (or throwing Oil first) inflicts Burn, which staggers it for even longer. Other fire tools work the same way.

Clear the two Ashina swordsmen at the bottom of the stairs first and mind the spear-bearer on the hill to the left (Gachiin's Sugar helps take him out quietly), then land a stealth deathblow on the ogre while it is still chained. If it breaks free, you can still get a second stealth deathblow by running back to Anayama the peddler, waiting for it to lose you, and crouch-walking back along the wall.

In the fight, all its normal attacks can be run around, dodged late, or deflected. The Perilous Grab and Perilous Lunge are the killers: jump or dash backwards or run around it, and never land more than about three hits after an attack or you will not get away in time. The Loaded Umbrella blocks its grabs. After many consecutive hits it stays staggered for a while, and Ungo's Sugar softens its heavy damage. If it throws you off the cliff, grapple a branch or the ogre itself immediately.
