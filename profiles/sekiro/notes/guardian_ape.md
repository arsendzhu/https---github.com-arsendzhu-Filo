# Guardian Ape
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_Ape
tags: boss, sunken valley, beast, terror, prosthetic

The Guardian Ape is a mandatory boss deep in the Sunken Valley, guarding the Lotus of the Palace in a shallow pond. It has one deathblow in each of its two phases, and the second phase plays like a different boss.

Phase 1 is a beast fight, so the Shinobi Firecracker works: it frightens the ape and creates openings. When it roars to intimidate you, a grapple point appears on its head; grappling to it stuns it briefly. Its dung throw is very telegraphed (the camera zooms out as it leaps), the projectile can be deflected in mid-air or on the ground, and the ape is vulnerable for a couple of seconds afterwards while it holds its hand to its bottom. Do not stand behind it while it sits down: it releases a poisonous fart that applies Poison almost instantly. Its running leap grab covers huge distance with a large hitbox; running away is far safer than trying to jump it. The ground-scrape grab can be jumped over for stagger damage.

Phase 2 begins right after the first deathblow: the ape picks up its head and the sword from its neck. Its scream (it puts the head back on and howls) deals damage and builds Terror over a huge radius, more in front than behind, so get behind it or well away. A Pacifying Agent resets Terror build-up and raises Terror resistance for 30 seconds, and the Mottled Purple Gourd helps the same way. The key trick: deflect its big Charged Overhead slam so it tumbles, then use the Loaded Spear's drag on the headless neck to pull the centipede partly out for massive posture damage. Deflecting the overhead also topples its balance on its own.
