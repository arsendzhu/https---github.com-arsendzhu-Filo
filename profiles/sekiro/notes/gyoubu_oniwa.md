# Gyoubu Masataka Oniwa
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Gyoubu_Masataka_Oniwa
tags: boss, ashina outskirts, horse, grapple, firecracker

Gyoubu is the first mandatory boss, fought on horseback in the battlefield before the Ashina Castle Gate, with two deathblows. You are still weak here, so bring Ungo's Sugar and Pellets.

The fight rewards the grappling hook: whenever he does his turning charge or swings the spear on its rope, a grapple point appears and you can pull yourself onto him and attack; the Grappling Hook Attack combat art makes that even better, and he always follows the turn charge with a spin-around swing if you are on his left. You get free hits while he introduces himself at the start, and stunning him in place with a Shinobi Firecracker before he charges off extends that.

The Shinobi Firecracker (unlocked by giving Robert's Firecrackers, sold by the Memorial Mob merchant in the Ashina Outskirts, to the Sculptor) is the star tool: it frightens the horse for a while and deals big posture damage, though it has a cooldown. He also has low Burn resistance, so two Flame Vent blasts stun him. His attacks do little posture damage, so deflecting everything is a valid plan.
