# Posture and deflecting
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Combat
tags: combat, posture, deflect, mikiri counter, deathblow

Sekiro's fights are won on Posture, not just vitality. Every enemy has a posture bar that fills when you deflect their attacks (press block just as the hit lands) or hit them; when it fills, they are open to a deathblow. Posture recovers faster while an enemy has lots of vitality, so chipping vitality makes posture damage stick. Turtling behind a held block still lets your own posture break, so deflect rather than block, and keep attacking to stop their posture from regenerating.

Perilous attacks flash a red kanji and cannot be blocked: thrusts are answered with the Mikiri Counter (press dodge the moment the thrust lands to stomp the blade for big posture damage; it is the first skill in the Shinobi Arts tree and needs the Shinobi Esoteric Text), sweeps are jumped (kicking off the enemy's head adds posture damage), and grabs must be dodged or jumped away from.

Status effects to respect: Burn drains about ten percent of vitality over ten seconds and halts posture recovery (Dousing Powder cures it); Poison ticks for forty seconds (Antidote Powder); Terror from screams and Headless-type enemies kills outright when its gauge fills (Pacifying Agent resets it and raises resistance for thirty seconds).
