# Lady Butterfly
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Butterfly
tags: boss, hirata estate, optional, illusions, snap seed

Lady Butterfly is an optional two-phase boss (one deathblow each) in the Hidden Temple under the Hirata Estate audience chamber. To reach her you need the Hidden Temple Key from Owl and to get past Juzou the Drunkard. Beating her is needed to finish the Young Lord's Bell Charm memory.

Phase 1: she yells before her sweep attack, deflects a couple of hits and counters, throws kunai, and constantly leaps onto the wires. The Loaded Shuriken punishes her every time she jumps onto a wire, and the Shinobi Firecracker, Flame Vent or Fistful of Ash stun her for an opening. Her drop kick from the wires and her plunge (which wrestles you down) are the moves to respect. After the first deathblow the fight reveals you were fighting an illusion.

Phase 2 adds magic: butterfly projectiles on her kunai throws and waltz, a frenzied flurry of slashes ending in a Perilous Sweep, and an illusion spell that summons endless mist enemies. A Snap Seed dispels those illusions; if you leave them, she turns the remaining ones into homing butterfly projectiles. Jump over the Perilous Sweep (Senpou Leaping Kicks turns that into heavy posture damage), and Ungo's Sugar and Pellets help survive her damage if your vitality is still low.
