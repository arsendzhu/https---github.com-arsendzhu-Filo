# Headless Ape
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Headless_Ape
tags: boss, ashina depths, optional, terror, prosthetic

The Headless Ape is an optional boss with two vitality bars, found in the Ashina Depths after the Guardian Ape of the Sunken Valley is defeated. Its first phase is the Guardian Ape's second phase again, so the same tactics apply: stay away from the Terror-building growl (Pacifying Agent or the Mottled Purple Gourd help, and the Phoenix's Lilac Umbrella nullifies both the damage and the Terror), deflect the Charged Overhead so the body tumbles, then use the Loaded Spear's drag on the neck to pull out the centipede for big vitality and posture damage. Use a Ceremonial Tanto before the fight so you have plenty of Spirit Emblems for prosthetics.

After the first deathblow it howls to call a brown female ape that fights like a weaker Guardian Ape with part of the phase-one moveset. She is much weaker than the boss, so rush her down first: the Shinobi Firecracker and Flame Vent stagger her and open her up, and the Mortal Draw combat art hurts her a lot. She mostly stays passive and attacks after one of the Headless Ape's combos has been deflected.
