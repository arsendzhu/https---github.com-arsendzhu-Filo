# Prosthetic tools
source: https://sekiro-shadows-die-twice.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Firecracker
tags: prosthetic, firecracker, loaded spear, snap seed, items

Prosthetic tools are fitted by giving the found item to the Sculptor at the Dilapidated Temple, and each use costs Spirit Emblems. The Shinobi Firecracker comes from Robert's Firecrackers, sold by the Memorial Mob merchant in the Ashina Outskirts: it scatters a flash of gunpowder that easily frightens beasts, so it staggers animals such as Gyoubu's horse, the Guardian Ape and dogs, and its Spring-load and Long Spark upgrades widen or lengthen the effect.

The Loaded Spear comes from Gyoubu's Broken Horn, found in a chest in the Ashina Reservoir gatehouse (needs the Gatehouse Key). It thrusts at mid range, and pressing the prosthetic button again drags the enemy toward you, strips loose armour from heavy foes, and pulls the centipede out of the Headless Ape after a deflected overhead slam.

The Flame Vent, from the Flame Barrel in the Hirata Estate, scares red-eyed enemies and builds Burn (more with Oil thrown first). Snap Seeds are quick items, not prosthetics: they break active illusions and damage phantom enemies, up to ten carried, sold by the Dungeon Memorial Mob and found around the Hirata Estate and Ashina Outskirts. Pacifying Agents reset Terror build-up.
